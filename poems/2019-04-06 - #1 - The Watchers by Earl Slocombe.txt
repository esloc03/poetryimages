The Watchers by Earl Slokum.
It was trash day
great,
another chore to add to the list.
It was dark out,
a cold October night.
When I was out there,
the smell of dog mess filled my nostrils.
The smell of weak old rotten beans and rice and cheese.
The weekly grind,
the sound of plastic being pulled across the pathway,
the sound of nothing,
the sound of no one.
Nobody cares.
But still,
I could feel them watching me.
They're always watching me.
The moon,
the clouds,
the wind.
Yes,
even the wind watches me.
