At last, the bell rang,
and everyone went away.
I was here,
and they were there—
the distance between us clear,
a separation,
the cold space between me and them,
between you and me,
like dried fruit.
What I'd give to go back...
to reach for your hand.
Back then, I was afraid—
afraid you wouldn't reach back,
afraid that you wouldnt stand against them with me.

So now I stand alone
because I was afraid.
I'm no longer afraid,
but I miss you.
