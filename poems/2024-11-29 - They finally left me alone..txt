They finally left me alone.
It feels different now.
Dare I say it feels good?
The weight has lifted.
The dark clouds, no more.

After all these years of struggling,
of suffering,
of feeling alone,
you're still here.

Your eyes are flowers in God's garden,
and your voice melts the ice around my heart
for the first time in what feels like forever.
I can hear you.

For this, I am thankful,
and I will always love you,
my flowery one.
