Something is hitting me
And it hurts
In my eye, my face, my cheek.
Something pulls my arms
This way, then that.
Something squeezes my chest —
Heavy. Strong.
Something hurts.

The dog is here,
My good old dog.
And winter is coming.
I can feel it
Whispering.

Without a rope,
I think I’ll die out here.
Without a rope,
I think I’ll die.

Don’t do it,
it whispers.

Keep going,
it whispers again.

I’m tired,
I say.

I know,
it answers.
But you’re still here.
