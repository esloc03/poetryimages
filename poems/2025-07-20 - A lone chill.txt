A lone chill
A plastic chair
A flickering light
A moldy bread

If you're going to do it—
just...
do it.

I'm tired.
Of keeping this alive.
I'm so tired.

We've been here before.
And we will be here again.

Nothing else matters
except for you.

In every moment
while I'm thinking about you,
I lose track
of the amount of times
I think about you.

As I recall
every wrinkle upon your face—
I forget your face.

Your kind, loving face.
Your faceless,
kind, loving face.

Your smell
of everything
and of nothing.

Oh
to picture you again.
To smell you.
To hold you.
To love you,
again.
