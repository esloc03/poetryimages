The Goldfish

There is a goldfish
A winsickle thing
A goldfish who dares
To believe he can sing

And he can

He blasts out a sweet little tune
About the tinklinking stars
And a beautiful moon

About how he wishes
He wasn't all alone
He sings in a high pitch
Mototonical tone

But the sound that he sings
Cracks the glass
And a maracaraclus thing
Comes to pass

The water rushed outward
It moved like a blast
And the walls of his world
Were shattered at last

He drifted on currents
So wide and so free
Where the tank dissolves
Into the open sea

Now his song fills the deep
Where the whales are home
He sings to the ocean
And is never alone
