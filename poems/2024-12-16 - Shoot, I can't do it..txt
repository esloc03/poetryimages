Shoot, I can't do it.
I'm judging everything I write.
I delete almost every word.
I know I just need to keep quiet
and let my fingers get through to the keys.

But I'm trying to be so poetic.
And finally, I realize
that life itself is poetic.

Just by writing these few sentences here,
it is enough.

And maybe saying how beautiful you look tonight,
with your hair cap.
It's amazing how long
we have been together in this world.

You make my life easier.

Every day, I am more afraid
that we are one day closer
to our last.

But tonight,
with you by my side,
I remember:
love like this
never truly ends.

...my best friend.
