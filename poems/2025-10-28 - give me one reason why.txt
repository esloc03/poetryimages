give me one reason why 
I have a heavy heart 

Why I smile 
when I feel so alone 

Why words turn to water 
and fall from my eyes 

Why every sound 
cuts through me 

It hurts to live 
and it hurts to die 

but I am here, breathing 
in the jaws of life 
in the clutches of death 

A slow step 
along the thin line 
between good and evil 

There are no words 
for how it feels 
Only silence 
and a cup of coffee 
growing cold beside me.
