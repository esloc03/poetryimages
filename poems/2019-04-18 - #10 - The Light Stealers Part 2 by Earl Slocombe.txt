The light-steelers,
part two,
by Earl Slurcom.
The dog sleeps,
the wife sleeps,
the world sleeps.
I do not sleep.
I do not sleep easy knowing what I know.
They are out there.
They are just waiting for me to think that they are gone.
They are just waiting until I drop my guard so they can knock me down.
They are watching.
They are still watching me.
The dog sleeps,
the wife sleeps,
the world sleeps.
I cannot sleep easy knowing that they are out there.
