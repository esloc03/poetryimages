The Talking Tree by Earl Slugum There,
standing in front of me,
was a tree I'd been talking to.
Its beautiful broad branches reaching out into the sky.
It looked like a spider's web,
the way the branches intertwined so perfectly.
I stood there,
gazing in wonder at its magnificence.
Hello,
I said.
Hello,
tree.
The tree said nothing.
Hello,
tree.
I said again.
The tree weaved in a wind.
How are you,
old friend?
Again,
the tree stared blankly and said nothing.
How are you,
tree?
I said once more.
Why won't you speak to me?
A few moments passed,
and the tree said nothing.
Goodbye,
tree.
A strong wind blew.
The tree weaved goodbye.
