It's madness, it's all madness.

They cry for nothing.

Their tiny blue faces, and tiny blue hands clutch on to life in spite of it all.

While we sit on our thrones and spit on their children.
Eating processed foods and drinking processed wines.

We laugh as they beg, laugh, indeed as they beg for nothing.

They are crying for mercy!

It hurts to watch them cry.. what have you done? In my name, what do you continue to do?

You heartless monster…

You soulless devil.

When will you end their suffering?
