My finger hurts.
I think I chewed off my fingernail.
It's throbbing and hurting which I know I already said but the matter is pressing.
Poetry itself is freedom.
If I want to I can rhyme, all the time.
And if I want to not rhyme
Then I can do that
And if I want to curse
Or talk some crap about something
Or talk about some nonsensical bird with seventeen eyes with his silver patches and beak that speaks 2000 languages and eats children
I can do that too
That is why I like to write poetry
Perhaps it's the closest we get to free will
The closest we get to a God
Truly, I can write anything
Weather it offensive or defensive
Or ugly or fat
Or blonde
Worst case scenario is nobody ever reads it
Best case scenario is everybody reads it
Everybody loves it
I make money
Publishers make money
Everybody talks a lot about it
And then as you all know everybody dies
Everybody forgets
Everybody ends up in the grave
Either way
Right now in this moment,
I'm truly absolutely free
