The Earth called out to me.
Keep going.
Continue your work.
Its voice echoed through my mind.
I've been dwelling on it recently,
wondering what those words meant.
And then it hit me.
It literally hit me.
A great big arm grew from the dirt,
and handed me this glimmering silver pen,
and then the hand flicked my forehead and knocked me unconscious.
I woke up an hour ago under a tree,
with a newfound passion for writing.
I write so much that I'm tired,
but I can't stop.
The voice in my mind reminds me why I'm here.
To write.
To write as a,
I have some important message to share.
Like hell I do,
I'm just trying to complete my one page,
a day journal.
In hopes that someone,
somewhere,
finds something inside that I don't see.
Something,
especially that I didn't put there intentionally.
I hope this is worth something.
