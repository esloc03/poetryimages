It was October.
October the 20-something.
It was dark outside.
There weren't many stars in the sky.
I was outside for one reason.
I was outside to take the dog for a crap.
The air was cold and the night was still.
While the dog was walking around,
trying to find a place that smelled good to dump his load,
I was observing the world.
Just observing.
And from the corner of my eye,
I thought I saw something under the tree.
I did.
I did see something.
Something dark.
And honestly,
it scared me.
