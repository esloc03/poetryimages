For some reason, the music isn't hitting the spot tonight.  
It's like my soul is done with writing,  
like my mind is disgusted by it once again.  

But I wanted to write something,  
and if this is all I write, then so be it.  
At least I wrote something.  

To all you writers out there struggling,  
I doubt you're reading this, but if you are,  
I know you've been here before.  
Some of you gave up because it didn't feel right.  
But as long as you keep hitting those keys,  
something will come out. It has to.  
And if it doesn't, share it anyway.  

Don't rub it in people's faces,  
but leave it written on the newspaper on the train  
or on a sticky note at the dentist's office.  
Leave it somewhere, and let the world know.  

Somewhere out there is a beautiful writer.  
Somewhere out there is someone who writes  
the most intense nonsensical poems about cabbages.  
And then go home, sit down, and let it go.
