As my light begins to grow.
As my productivity increases.
As my depression fades.

They grow.

With each word I write.
With each day that passes.

They grow.

And right at the gates of freedom.
When I am on the brink of success.

They’ll appear.

The light stealers.

And when they appear, my light will be gone.

So if they do appear. 

Promise me.

Promise me you’ll help me.
