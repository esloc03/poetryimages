You see them everyday.
They’re dead.
They have no light inside of them.
They have no fire.

Their eyes have lost the spark.
Their hearts have lost the love.
They have nothing anymore.
They have only emptiness.

Beside them you walk.

Don’t let them see your light.
Don’t let them take your fire.
Don’t let your eyes will lose their spark.
Don’t let your heart lose its love.

Let them take nothing from you.
