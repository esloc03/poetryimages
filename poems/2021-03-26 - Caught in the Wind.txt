The air fills the night and together the cricket sings and the fireflies ignite the empty space around me.
Their blue and yellow flames comfort me as I sit alone.
The stars are not needed now and the moon hasn't shown his face.
The wind brushes against my legs and asks me to dance and as I dance he embraces me his hands rundown my spine and I tremble.
My breath escapes me my body spins like summer's the blades of grass during our dance and the orange leaves crinkle and crack beneath my feet.
The wind whistles and howls as we dance together then a bright light catches my attention my foot folds on its side and I fall on the ground face down.
The wind passes over me the cricket sings and the fireflies continue their dance and the moon laughs his face brighter than a thousand stars.
