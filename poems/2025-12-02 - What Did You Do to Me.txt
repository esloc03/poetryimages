There isn't a day that goes by that I don't think of you.
You make me feel seen in a way I didn't even realize I needed.
And now there's this ache for you
a constant, heavy ache,
something I keep trying to push down,
but it doesn't budge.

The sun still shines within me, somehow,
even though I'm a potato,
a sad lump of clay,
a slug on the bottom of a ledge.

What did you do to me?
How did you slip past every wall I ever built,
and leave me feeling like this
raw, wanted and ruined, all at the same time?
