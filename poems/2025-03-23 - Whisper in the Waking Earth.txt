There is a whisper in the waking earth,
a breath of magic,
a spark of birth.
There is a voice upon the air,
a voice at lingers everywhere.
There is a shiver in the swaying trees,
a secret carried on the breeze.
There is a stirring in the ground,
a hidden pulse,
a silent sound.
There is something deep within my heart,
something ancient,
something dark.
There is something in the silent night,
in the fading glow of dying light.
There is something whispering my name in the ember,
in the flame.
There is something here with me,
a presence watching,
patiently.
Something only I can tell,
in the darkness I know him well,
something in my heart I know 
this is my time to go.
Something on the morrow you'll find,
the motionless corpse I left behind.
