They said it will be over soon and I decided to believe them.
Now here we are,
eleven years later.
The system is still broken.
The message is still lost.
The hearts and the souls of many are burning.
And there are so many people out there longing for something,
searching,
calling out,
crawling around,
living,
dying,
being eaten alive by their own choices.
And perhaps I'm one of the lucky ones,
neck pain and an ear infection.
I am blessed.
I am only blessed 
and I am not grateful enough.
