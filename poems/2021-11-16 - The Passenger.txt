I am a passenger here.
Actually,
I am the only passenger.
You are the passenger too.
We are destined to live out our days in this seemingly pointless cycle of birth,
suffering,
mild happiness.
No,
brief happiness,
temporary at best.
And finally,
we welcome death.
The only thing that life cannot take away from us,
we are all guaranteed that.
Even I cannot take death away from you.
You,
me,
we are all the same thing,
innocent and helpless.
I try not to listen to the others with their 12 steps to life and its meaning in the order and in the chaos.
But sometimes,
even that relieves the infinite suffering that is boredom.
Cheer up this here.
It may never happen.
It already is happening,
I say.
That's all there is.
