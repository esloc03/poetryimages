Most people don't even know that I exist.
The world doesn't have time for me.
Think about that.

From my perspective,
I'm the most important person in the world.

From someone else's perspective,
I'm the most insignificant.

I'd be lucky to be an extra in the background.

How incredible is it when someone acknowledges you?

There are 7.753 billion people in the world,
but in my world,
including all of those "extra" people,
doctors,
janitors,
store clerks,
Elon Musk,
You.

There are probably only 1000 people.

And how many of that 1000 people can I say are truly important?

Maybe 30…
