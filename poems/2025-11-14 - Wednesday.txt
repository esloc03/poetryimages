It's Wednesday,
somewhere between waking and sleeping,
somewhere between day and night.
The floorboards whisper beneath my feet,
and while snow falls outside,
snow falls inside.
My face,
a snowy blur,
my eyes glass,
my heart heavy,
broken.
And all around me,
the world melted into white,
a quiet storm pressing against my chest.
Hunger's stricken,
I reached out,
I called into the abyss,
and it called back.
Mayor dogs,
boats of meat and cream,
and I ate twelve of them before melting into nothingness.
