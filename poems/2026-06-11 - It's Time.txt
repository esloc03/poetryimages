It's Time

I sit down
Open a notepad
And I write
Not because I want to
Because it won't stop whispering
"The magazines need you"
It digs its claws in my neck
Every time I hesitate
It would be easier if I had a drink
But my doctor said I shouldn't
So I sit with the silence
And it booms in my skull
I fucking love it...
And now it is asleep.
