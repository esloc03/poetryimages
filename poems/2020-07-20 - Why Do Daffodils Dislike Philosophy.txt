Beside rivers of flowing cheese,
and fish made of chalk.
Cabbages and daffodils
blissfully talk.

Under the wooden sky.
In the endless dark,
they talk about philosophy,
while they stroll through the park.

The cabbage is a stoic
with a bright green face,
and big green leaves,
all over the place.

The daffodil is wild.
He believes he's a cat
with a bright green tail,
and a yellow hat.

"No" said the cabbage.
"Because you have no paws
and speaking on that…" he said.
"You have no claws."

But the daffodil was right,
because he was wise,
and because neither
he or the cabbage
had any eyes.
