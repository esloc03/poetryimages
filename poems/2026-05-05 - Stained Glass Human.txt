Broken people are like shattered glass
not just sharp,
they cut deep.

They've been dropped,
walked over,
thrown away like trash.

But look closer
past the fractures,
past the hands that won't stop shaking.

There's something there
a flicker,
a stubborn kind of light
that refuses to die.

It slips through a crooked smile,
hides behind tired eyes,
or breaks loose in a laugh
That echoes in your heart

But if you pick them up
even if it means bleeding too,

And choose not to let go.

Make sure you gather every jagged piece.
And don't flinch when it hurts.
And don't turn away when it's ugly.

you don't just fix what's broken

you build something that only exists
because it was shattered,

something that catches the light
in ways nothing whole ever could

a stained glass human
