The smell of burnt toast seeps through the crack in the door,
crawling through the house,
filling every crevice with desperation, anger, and sadness.

Black stains on the rug—
the coffee mug’s final leap,
its shattered remains soaking into the silence.

The quiet cry of a sock under the bed,
lost, forgotten,
alone, forever.

And not forgetting the glorious yell of victory
from the last of the bananas,
standing bruised and proud.

The real MVP.
