There standing in front of me was the tree I’d been talking to.

It’s beautiful broad branches reaching out into the sky. 

It looked like a spider’s web the way the branches intertwined so perfectly. 

I stood there in it’s gazing in wonder at its magnificence.

“Hello” I said. “Hello Tree”
The tree said nothing.

“Hello tree!” I said again.

The tree waved in the wind.

“How are you, old friend?”

Again the tree stared blankly and said nothing.
