The musical notes glisten in my ears.
I hear words,
soft whispers in my head.
I write them down,
and then I read them to you,
and you seem to like them.
Now I am addicted to it,
the way you smile.
These aren't my words.
I didn't write this.
I am only a vessel,
and I wish I could be more.
I wish that we could be more.
