The sun has come and gone,
and the wind has drawn its last breath across this lonely planet.
Lovers have been and gone,
souless beings wonder,
the shattered wasteland I once called home,
crushed by an unknown force.
The earth lies still,
the Lord is angered,
and I have turned away from him.
Or perhaps he has turned away from me.
I am no longer worthy.
I do not ask that he take away my suffering.
