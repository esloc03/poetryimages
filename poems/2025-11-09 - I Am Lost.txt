Lord,
I am coming home.
Make a room for me.
That I may rest my weary soul.
Lord,
I am coming home.
Bake bread for me.
That I may eat and be made whole.
Set a seat beside you,
Lord,
so we may sit together once again.
I beg forgiveness from your heart.
I beg to feel your love again.
Once more,
Lord,
lift me up from the dust where I have lain.
I pray,
O Lord,
when I ring the bell,
the door stands open and you welcome me in.
I am lost,
Lord,
though you are with me,
I am lost.
