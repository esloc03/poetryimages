The birds,
with their orange beaks and beautiful brown wings play together outside the window.
The trees watch them and I watch the trees,
and the roses dance with the wind,
and the crows fly around in the dreamless sky above,
and the greens and browns and whites and blues,
they all melt together under a wooden ball of flame,
all of us not knowing,
not caring,
living and dying at the same time together.
