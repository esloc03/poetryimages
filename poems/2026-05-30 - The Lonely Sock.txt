I found a lonely crusty old sock
In a wooden box
He has a picture of a yellow dog
Printed on him
And he looks sad without his partner
I wonder what happened
Did time take her away from him?
Did it sneak up on them on a random Wednesday at the beach house?

That's just the way of things
The sock seemed to whisper...

I closed the lid and put the box back on the shelf where I'd found it
