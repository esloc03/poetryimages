Super speed would be my superpower,
so maybe I could fix it all.

Fix the broken neon lights in the cities,
the ones that scream in quiet desperation,
their flickering messages only broken hearts can read—
their hunger, their loneliness,
a reflection of the cities that house them.

The cameras don’t capture it.
The news articles don’t get it right.
The books, the movies, the late-night TV shows,
the reality shows we watch—
how can they be so fucking blind?

There are people falling apart right in front of their bright plastic eyes.
Every. Single. Day.
And yet, you stare at us like we’re an illness,
no better than trash.

Well, from where I’m standing,
you’re a echo of everything this city has become,
said the poet to the neon sign.
