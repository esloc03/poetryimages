It's getting hard to breathe out here.
The people are getting restless.
The streets look more like deserts.
Oceans of loneliness.
The grass has melted.
The bees have long since gone.
The moon, a saltine cracker.
Clouds of fire light the sky.

Just plastic trees and plastic hearts.
