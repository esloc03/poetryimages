Here’s to the mornings
When I open my eyes.
The light hurts.
Life hurts.
But there’s work to be done.

And we do the work.
And we make the coffee.
And we forget about the coffee.
And now the coffee is too cold.

Here’s to those kinds of mornings
When the last banana is the highlight,
When there’s a knife in your soul.

Those mornings.
Knife-in-your-soul mornings.

Good-to-be-alive mornings.
