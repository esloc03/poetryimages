I don't know what to write about.

I feel like I've written about everything.

Like there are no more ways to describe
pain and sorrow.

I've compared it to a grilled cheese,
a lump of shit stuck on the toilet bowl,
a banana slowly turning rotten on the kitchen counter.

I've dragged it through metaphor
and left it out in the open to spoil
where everyone can see.

And it still sits there smiling at me,
waiting for a time when I write about it again.
