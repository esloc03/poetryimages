I remember thinking
the painting on the wall
was crooked

As water fell from the ceiling
dripping in a strange rhythm

I watched the drops disappear
into the puddle on the floor

...I remember the face
of the girl in the painting

Her speckles
Her wide angled eyes
Her glowing smile

A smile warm enough
to soften devils

...I still see her
when I close my eyes

She held a baby
lovingly in her arms

Most would assume
it was God

But the baby was Satan
and she was his mother

And when I saw it clearly

the paint began to melt

Her face ran down the canvas
and melted into my mind
Forever
