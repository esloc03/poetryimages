I saw a fish today  
It sat there in the middle of a field, it was as big as a small car,  
smoke spilled from its mouth... I just drove right by it...
I should've turned the car around, but I was late for work and couldn't risk another warning.  
So I left that magical fish behind,  
I watched it fade in the rearview mirror.  
After my shift, I went back,  
but the field was empty,  
Except for a small bowl of cold soup.
I wondered for a while if I'd ever see the magic again but eventually I forgot about it.
