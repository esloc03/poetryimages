There are times when I look at you
And everything falls into place.
There are times when I look at you
And you give me grace.
There are times when I look at you
And I want to scream.
There are times when I look at you
And I’m living the dream.

There are times when I look at you
And I feel so small.
There are times when I look at you
And I have it all.

And through everything —
Every moment —
I’ll be here.
Always.
