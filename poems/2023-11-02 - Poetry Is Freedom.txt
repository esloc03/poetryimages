My finger hurts.
I think I chewed off my finger nail.
It's throbbing and hurting,
which I know I already said but the matter is pressing.
Poetry itself is freedom.
If I want to,
I can rhyme all the time.
And if I want to not rhyme,
then I can do that too.
And if I want to curse,
or talk some crap about something,
or talk about some nonsensical bird with 17 eyes and silver patches and a beak that speaks 2000 languages and each children I can do that too.
That is why I like to write poetry.
Perhaps it's the closest we get to free will.
The closest we get to a good,
truly I can write anything,
whether it be offensive or defensive.
Or ugly or fat or blonde.
Worst case scenario is nobody ever reads it.
Best case scenario,
everybody reads it.
Everybody loves it.
I make money,
publishers make money.
Everybody talks a lot about it.
And then as you all know,
everybody dies.
Everybody forgets.
Everybody ends up in the grave.
Either way,
right now,
in this moment,
I'm truly,
absolutely free.
