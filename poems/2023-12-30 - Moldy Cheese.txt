As I stood there in the long grass,
gazing at the magnificence of the world,
as the blades of grass grazed my bare skin.
The cheese started to grow mold in the fridge,
and the dogs wondered when I'd be home.
I'd left the lights on,
and the music playing.
I knew I'd be gone for a while,
but it's only now as I stare into the river,
ready to jump,
that I realize how much I miss you.
